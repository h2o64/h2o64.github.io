My paper [Diffusion-based Annealed Boltzmann Generators: Benefits, Pitfalls and Hopes](https://openreview.net/forum?id=la4FDaeIbw), co-authored with [Maxence Noble](https://maxencenoble.github.io/), has been accepted at TMLR 2026! I'll be presenting it at the [SIAM Conference on Mathematics of Data Science](https://www.siam.org/conferences-events/siam-conferences/mds26/) (MDS26), as part of the minisymposium *"Sampling Meets Machine Learning: Design, Analysis, and Applications,"* in Salt Lake City, Utah (November 16–20, 2026).
